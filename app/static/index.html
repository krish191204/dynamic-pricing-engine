Price the next week

    1 of 8 · The product

    Which product is this?

    Name it, then give its sales. A file, a pasted table, or the real UCI sample.

    Ticking the example fills stock 400, cost 1.20, 8 weeks, a market price of 2.55, and a 15% sales fall. The file is a real extract of StockCode 85123A from the UCI Online Retail set. Title rows are skipped. The code has to match a code in the file.

    2 of 8 · The stock

    What do you hold?

    Stock, what one unit costs you, and how soon unsold stock loses its value.

    3 of 8 · The market

    What is the market doing?

    A price you can see now, and your guess of how far sales fall if your price rises 10%. After the run we also read that same idea off your weeks, and show both.

    The suggested price still uses the percent you type, so you can check it with a calculator. Leave the last box empty when the file already has a price or a sales amount.

    4 of 8 · Sales

    What sold

    Each bar is one week. The latest week is marked Latest.

    5 of 8 · Forecast

    Next week

    The mean of the last four weeks, written out, and how far that average missed the latest week.

    6 of 8 · Price

    What to charge

    The number is what to charge. Under it is the arithmetic. Move the slider to see what another price would do. Nothing in the shop changes.

    7 of 8 · Stock

    How long the stock lasts

    Cover is the stock you typed, divided by next week’s forecast. The working is on this step.

    8 of 8 · Trust

    Can you trust the file?

    Rows that were dropped, the miss on the latest week, and a factor-desk score of this product’s weeks.
